02 / ABOUT

A cross-disciplinary
research perspective.

Dr. Bahar Farahani is an Assistant Professor at Shahid Beheshti University and received her Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from the University of Tehran.

Her research spans artificial intelligence, privacy-preserving and federated learning, big data analytics, AIoT, smart and connected health, and computer architecture, including custom instruction design and hardware–software co-design. Her recent work also explores agentic AI and space computing.

Her research laboratory, FARA Intelligence Laboratory (Future Architectures for Trustworthy and Autonomous Intelligence Laboratory), brings these interests together around trustworthy and autonomous intelligence.

She has authored or co-authored more than 60 scholarly publications and book chapters and has served in international journal and conference editorial and leadership roles. Her academic and industry work includes data-driven solutions in healthcare, smart infrastructure, industrial IoT, and AI-integrated platforms.
