# Dr. Bahar Farahani — one-page academic website

A responsive, single-page academic website with a blue visual theme, dark/light mode, a portrait, a unified Research Areas section, and a comprehensive overview of academic service and activities.

## Run / publish

Open `index.html` directly in a browser, or deploy the entire folder as static content to GitHub Pages, Netlify, or any static web host. No build tool, paid service, or dependencies required.

## Contents

- `index.html` — text, research keywords, service entries, books, awards, talks, teaching, innovation, and all links.
- `styles.css` — layout, responsive styles, and dark/light palettes.
- `script.js` — menu, light/dark theme toggle, and current copyright year.
- `bahar-farahani.jpg` — portrait supplied for the website.
- `favicon.svg` — abstract interconnected-node icon (no BF monogram).

**There is no CV PDF in this package.** No download link to the PDF appears on the page, and no publication list is maintained in the code. The Publications link and Google Scholar buttons direct readers to Google Scholar.

## Source and pre-publication checks

The professional roles, awards, talks, teaching, and research summary were derived from the user-supplied CV. The two edited-book records were cross-checked with Springer Nature, and the Amazon product URLs and LinkedIn URL were provided by the user.

Before publication, obtain Bahar's approval for her portrait and biographical copy, confirm years and current appointments, and check the precise wording of award and distinction claims. Additional research keywords express relevant methods and potential directions; they are not assertions that Bahar has published independently in each listed subfield.
